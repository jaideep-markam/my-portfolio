The 4-Step Editing Engine

From raw multi-gigabyte footage to algorithm-ready exports in 24–48 hours.

STEP 01 Day 1

Raw Ingest & Hook Extraction

Drop footage into our dedicated high-speed cloud drive. I review all takes, identify the highest-converting soundbites, and script the opening pattern interrupt.

  • Secure cloud upload link
  • Best take selection & A-Roll assembly
  • Hook strategy formulation
STEP 02 Day 1–2

Dynamic Assembly & Motion Cut

Eliminating filler words, crafting J/L audio cuts, adding custom kinetic subtitles with branded typography, and inserting high-retention b-roll overlays.

  • Dead air elimination
  • Custom animated captions
  • Zoom framing & tracking graphics
STEP 03 Day 2

Color Science & Audio Foley

Applying professional DaVinci Resolve color grading for rich cinematic depth, matched skin tones, and precision multi-track sound design.

  • Film emulation LUT grading
  • Voice EQ & noise reduction
  • SFX risers, whooshes & hits
STEP 04 Same Day

Rapid Review & Master Delivery

First draft delivered via Frame.io for timestamped comments. We polish until you are 100% thrilled, then export multi-platform masters.

  • Unlimited sprint revisions
  • Platform-optimized bitrate (Reels/Shorts/TikTok)
  • Cover frame suggestions included
